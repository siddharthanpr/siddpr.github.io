Drona Privacy Policy

Drona is a personal strength-training app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Your data stays on your phone

Your workouts, notes and settings are stored on your phone. We do not run a server that receives them.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, Drona asks for one permission only: access to its own app-specific hidden folder in your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). The backup is written to your own Drive. It cannot see your other files, and nothing is sent to us.

Optional AI coach

If you turn on the AI coach, the text you choose to send is sent to the AI provider you configured, using your own API key. We do not receive it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics and no ads
  • We do not sell or share your data

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. Deleting the backup file from your Drive app data removes the backup.
